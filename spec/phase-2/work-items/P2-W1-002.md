# P2-W1-002 — PayPal Direct + Global Payout Controls

Owner: Worker 1
Depends on: P2-W1-001, P2-W2-001

Implement direct PayPal Connector and generic global payout controls.

Acceptance:
- PayPal Direct is independently represented from Stripe PayPal;
- merchant/account country eligibility is verified;
- PayPal client/app credentials and webhooks are vault-backed;
- refunds, payment status and provider evidence are reconciled;
- payout destination remains external and explicit;
- direct PayPal and Stripe PayPal are selectable only when their connected instances are eligible.
