# P4-W3-002 — Onchain FinancialOpportunity
Owner: worker-2
Dependencies: P4-W2-002, P4-W3-001

Extend FinancialOpportunity discovery to onchain opportunities.

Support evidence-backed observation of relevant opportunity families such as:
- liquidity;
- lending;
- staking;
- incentives;
- arbitrage/price differences;
- other policy-approved strategies.

Each opportunity must expose:
capital required, expected-return estimate, liquidity, fees, exit path, lock-up, smart-contract risk, oracle/bridge risk, maximum-loss bound where knowable, evidence freshness and policy eligibility.

Discovery/recommendation is never authorization. Do not claim guaranteed returns.

Acceptance:
- opportunities are searchable by the Lab;
- agent cannot execute outside the existing authorization path;
- provenance/evidence is preserved;
- adversarial and stale-observation cases are tested.
