Pay, Receive, Move, Convert, Checkout.

Operational areas:
Overview, Payments, Accounts, Activity, Opportunities, Connections, Capabilities, Security, Reports, Developers, Settings.

- merchant fiat-first payment model;
- crypto checkout;
- connection/auth UX;
- human-readable security warnings/blocks;
- test/live and testnet/mainnet clarity;
- empty/loading/error/success/UNKNOWN states;
- responsive accessibility.

Worker must use agent-browser for major journeys and verify real rendered behavior. No dead buttons, fake financial success or console errors.

Acceptance:
- desktop + mobile evidence;
- Stripe research mapped to concrete components/workflows;
- actual API/protocol wiring;
- no duplicate financial authority.
