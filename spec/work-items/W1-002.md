# W1-002 — Ledger, Reservations, Obligations and Clearing

Owner: Worker 1
Depends on: W1-001

Implement deterministic double-entry ledger, balance projections, reservations/holds, fulfillment activities, obligations and clearing records.

Acceptance:
- journal entries balance;
- projections rebuild from journal;
- reservations are atomic;
- obligations derive from activities/clearing;
- retries are idempotent;
- durable outbox/event publication exists.

No rail execution.
