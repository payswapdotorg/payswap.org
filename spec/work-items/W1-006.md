# W1-006 — Disputes, Recourse, Escrow, Bonds and Guarantees

Owner: Worker 1
Depends on: W1-005

Implement RecoursePolicy, DisputeCase, RecourseObligation and supported protection mechanisms.

Acceptance:
- transaction recourse policy is immutable after initiation;
- disputes do not rewrite original transactions;
- recourse produces separate obligations/effects;
- bonds/escrow are separately accounted;
- claim windows and evidence requirements are enforced.
