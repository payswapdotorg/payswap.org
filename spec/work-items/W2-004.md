# W2-004 — Lab, Director and Candidate Discovery

Owner: Worker 2
Depends on: W2-003

Implement Domain Packs, scenarios, simulation namespace, replay, evaluator, search plug-ins, candidate registry, shadow/canary promotion and Director control interfaces.

The Lab must treat certified smart-contract capabilities, ServiceAccessCapabilities, PSP connectors and participation mechanisms as searchable building blocks. For provider-backed capabilities, it searches ConnectedCapabilityInstance + CapabilityObservation rather than catalogue definitions alone, and may select provider-native optimization/recovery as an incumbent capability.

Acceptance:
- simulator cannot be called by production rail path;
- certified smart contracts can be composed into candidate Organizations without granting Lab authority;
- baseline suite exists;
- promotion is versioned;
- replay is reproducible;
- hard constraints precede optimization;
- PASS_THROUGH_NATIVE, COMPOSED_PAYSWAP and OPTIMIZED_MULTI_PROVIDER are evaluated as explicit candidates;
- provider-native capability candidates are not assumed inferior to PaySwap-composed candidates;
- Lab selection cannot mutate financial authority.
